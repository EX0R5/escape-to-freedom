# This is a J1 Summary Project
The game is set in the American South during the era of the Underground Railroad: a network of secret routes, safe houses, and trusted guides used by enslaved people seeking freedom. The tone is tense and suspenseful rather than fantastical, with descriptions focusing on dark forests, hidden trails, quiet farms, rivers, and the constant danger of being discovered. Most of the game takes place outdoors, although several buildings such as abandoned cabins, farmhouses, barns, and safe houses provide shelter and alternative routes.

Mechanically, it is a stealth-and-survival maze with multiple possible paths to freedom. The Player begins at a plantation and must find their own way through a network of forests, rivers, farms, roads, and hidden buildings before reaching a safe haven. Different routes provide different advantages and risks, and certain items such as maps, keys, boats, disguises, lanterns, and food can open new paths or make particular routes safer. The Player must avoid or confront hostile Creatures while navigating the maze. The Slave Catcher Captain serves as the main Monster and can pursue the Player through parts of the maze, creating an additional threat while the Player searches for a route to freedom. The other subbosses and monsters serve as a lighter, optional fight along the way, allowing the Player to encounter the combat and item systems before facing the final challenge.
